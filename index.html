The Story

From Damascus to Detroit.
Building Systems and Stories.

In 8th grade, I assembled my first computer from parts. By 17, I was Spacetoon's youngest employee -- "Mr. Radio" -- running programming for the biggest children's media network in the Middle East while still in high school.

That dual obsession never separated. At Spacetoon, I became Head of Sound, creating the audio identity for hundreds of Arabic-dubbed anime: Gundam Wing, Detective Conan, Beyblade. Meanwhile, I was building enterprise IT systems for international clients.

When the Syrian civil war erupted, I co-founded Radio Al-Kul in Istanbul -- building broadcast studios from scratch, training journalist teams, reporting from the ground. 500,000 daily listeners across seven provinces. NPR, Al Jazeera, and the Washington Post covered the story.

In Michigan, I founded SS Media Productions and Karazah Channel -- now 739K+ subscribers, 310M+ views, teaching Arabic to children worldwide. The Governor of Dubai recognized the work with the Mohammed Bin Rashid Award.

Today I manage healthcare IT for 18+ facilities with 99.8% uptime. I design sound for Academy Award films. I tour as FOH engineer with artists reaching millions. Sometimes all in the same week.

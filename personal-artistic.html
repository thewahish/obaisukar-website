Life Story

Obai Sukar's life is defined by transformation—transforming media, technology, and lives. Born in Syria, his journey took him across continents, building bridges between cultures through sound, media, and technology.

From the recording studios of Los Angeles to the broadcast facilities of the Middle East, from enterprise IT infrastructure in American healthcare to educational media reaching millions, his career spans two decades of relentless innovation.

"My mission has always been to use technology and media not just as tools, but as forces for positive change—to educate, to empower, and to give voice to the voiceless."

As a graduate of the Los Angeles Recording School, Obai honed his craft in sound engineering, earning recognition for his technical excellence. His work spans Academy Award-winning films, humanitarian broadcasting reaching millions, and IT systems protecting critical healthcare data.
